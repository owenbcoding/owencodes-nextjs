# Building LeadScout

Hi there! 👋

**LeadScout** came out of a problem I kept hitting myself: freelance prospecting is mostly admin. You search a town, open fifteen tabs, half-check whether each business is worth contacting, lose track of who you already emailed, and start again next week.

The tagline sums up the whole product: **"Find local businesses worth calling."**

**Live:** [leadscout.work](https://www.leadscout.work/)

## What it does

LeadScout lets you discover nearby businesses, prioritise the best leads, and organise your cold outreach in one place. The flow is deliberately three steps:

1. **Search a location** — pull the local businesses in an area
2. **Review and score them** — decide who's actually worth your time
3. **Export your leads** — take them straight into your outreach

It also tracks outreach status, so you don't email the same business twice and look like you're not paying attention.

## Who it's for

It's built for outbound-focused freelancers who need better local prospecting — plus small agencies, local service businesses, and sales teams doing the same work with a spreadsheet and good intentions.

## What I learned building it

### 1. The hard part is prioritisation, not data
Getting a list of businesses is easy. Deciding which twenty out of two hundred deserve a call is the thing people actually want help with, so scoring became the centre of the product rather than a nice-to-have.

### 2. One tool beats four tabs
Discovery, business context, prioritisation and outreach readiness were the four things I was doing in four different places. Collapsing them into one workflow was the whole value proposition — everything else is detail.

### 3. Ship the narrow version
It would have been easy to add a CRM, a mail sender, and a dashboard. It does local discovery and prioritisation well instead, and that constraint made it much easier to finish.

## What's Next?

The site is up and taking early-access signups ahead of the full launch. Next up is sharpening the scoring signals and smoothing the export-to-outreach handoff.
